Thank You For Being My Close Friend ❤️

Dear Haritha,

Honestly, I never thought that someone I had just met could become this close to me in such a short time. It's crazy that within just 48 hours, we went from barely knowing each other to talking, understanding, caring, and sharing things like we've known each other for so long.

What I really appreciate about you is the way you understand me without me having to explain everything. You care about the little things, you notice when something is wrong, and somehow you always make me feel comfortable being myself.

That kind of connection is really rare. Sometimes you meet someone and you just naturally click with them. No forcing conversations, no pretending to be someone else — everything just feels easy. Somehow, that's what happened with us.

Maybe 48 hours doesn't sound like a lot of time, but sometimes time doesn't decide how close two people can become. Some people take years to understand you, while some people just naturally get you.

And somehow, you became one of those people for me.

So, thank you. Thank you for being there, for caring, for listening, for understanding me, and simply for being my close friend.

I don't know where this friendship will take us, but I genuinely hope we keep making memories, annoying each other, supporting each other, laughing at stupid things, and being there for each other whenever we need it.

I'm really glad I met you. And honestly... thank you for becoming such a close and important friend to me. ❤️

Truly grateful for our friendship, From Talin ❤️
